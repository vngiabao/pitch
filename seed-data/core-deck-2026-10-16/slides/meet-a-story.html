~0:50 · Most people will never write an autobiography. Their lives aren’t empty. Trying to piece together a whole life from a blank page is an overwhelming place to begin. Your loved ones can tell their stories in their own voice over the phone. A Story is the AI biographer that calls them and helps them do that. The phone rings at the time they picked. They answer and talk. A Story asks the questions and listens to what they actually say. It follows the conversation instead of a fixed script. After each call, A Story keeps the original voice and transcript. It turns what they shared into a memory the family can revisit. That memory connects to the photos and letters around it, and to the people and dates. The interviewer remembers what’s already been shared. It picks up unfinished stories and lets each memory shape the next conversation. Their life is kept a little at a time, while they’re still living it. Calls and the archive are live today. So are photo uploads and billing. The home screen shows Rose, the example family in the app.
