~0:35 · Families care. They just keep missing the moment. Stories don’t come out on request. They come out when something sparks them. You’re around the table and see one old photo. “Wait, is that the lake house?” An hour of stories pours out. When you’re apart, that spark never happens. And when you do call, “so, tell me about your life” leads to a long pause. Where do you even begin? Life happens. You can’t keep finding time every day to get to know your parents. Some nights you just need to hear their voice. Imagine being one click away from their voice and stories, anywhere, any time. You may not always have time to call them. You shouldn’t have to wait for the perfect moment to feel close to them.
