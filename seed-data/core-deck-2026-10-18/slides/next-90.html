~0:45 · Next, we’re launching publicly and running the holiday gifting campaign. First, we’ll convert the five families already waiting. They’ll be our first paying families on the redesigned release. Second, we’ll launch into the gifting season. That means the App Store release and gift passes for adult children. It also means a first gifting push with the Hope College students who’ve shown interest. Third, we’ll prove families come back. We’ll track whether storytellers return for another call and whether relatives contribute. We’ll also track whether what they add shapes the next question. We’re planning our path to the first hundred through channels. We aren’t assuming a share of the market. For example, 1,000 qualified visits at 3% who buy and stay is 30 paying families. Those are example inputs. We’ll replace them with measured conversion. We expect families to stay beyond the memoir book because the call becomes a habit. There’s no homework. It comes at the time they chose. Every relative who joins adds their own version and a reason to keep going. Each call builds on the last because what the family adds shapes the next question. The longer a family uses A Story, the harder it is to replace. Years of voices and photos stay in one place, with the context around them. We’ll measure second-call rate and relatives contributing. We’ll also measure calls that use what the family added.
