~0:45 · Most current options fall into two categories: photo albums and memoir services. Albums keep the pictures. They lose the life behind them. Memoir services like Storyworth send weekly prompts that lead to a physical keepsake. What’s supposed to be a meaningful gift turns into homework for the parents. Habit research found a median of 66 days of daily repetition. Weekly prompts allow only about ten attempts in that time. Many only get through the first few before life gets busy and they stop. Once the book is printed, the purpose is gone. The book ends. Everyday life keeps going. That’s where most of a life’s stories happen. Source: Lally et al. 2010.
