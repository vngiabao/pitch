~0:30 · This is the core of how we think about it. Other products are projects with an ending: prompts, a book, done. A Story is a habit. The phone rings at the time they chose, and they just answer - no homework. And it is told from every side: Mom tells it one way, Uncle John adds his version, the grandkids add photos. Every call, photo and version adds to one living memory. A printed book is one chapter along the way, not the finish line.
