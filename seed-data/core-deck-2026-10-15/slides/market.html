Bottom-up. 132.7 million U.S. households at our $119 price is a $15.8 billion ceiling. We start with the 43.6 million households that include someone 65 or older: $5.19 billion a year. And the target that matters: at about $141 per account - an 80/20 mix of Individual and Family plans - roughly 710,000 paying accounts, just 1.6% of 65+ households, is a $100 million a year business. These are demographic ceilings, not validated demand; one paid account covers a whole family.
