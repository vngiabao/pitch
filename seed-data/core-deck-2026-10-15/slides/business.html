How we make money. First, subscription, because that is what keeps the story growing: families pay yearly for the ongoing interviews - under $10 a month: $119 a year for an individual, with one storyteller, 90 minutes of calls a month and a Classic Hardcover each year; or $229 a year, about $19 a month, for a family plan, with up to three storytellers and 200 shared minutes a month. Relatives read and contribute for free. There’s also a one-time Express gift pass at $79 and a $19.99 monthly plan. Second, books, whenever the family wants something physical - softcover from $39, Classic Hardcover $69, Heirloom $149. The book is a chapter, not the finish line. It’s bought as a gift, but kept as a habit: the adult child buys, the parent just answers the phone, and every relative who adds a memory gives the next call - and the next year - a reason. Renewal is the number we are proving next.
