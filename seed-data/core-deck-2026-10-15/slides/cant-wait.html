~0:40 · And it can’t wait. America is aging: 11,200 Americans turn 65 every day through 2027 - about 467 an hour, roughly 8 a minute - and by 2030 every baby boomer will be 65 or older. Nearly 1 in 5 Americans is already 65+, 64.6 million people. At the same time, families live further apart. And $124 trillion will pass between generations through 2048. Families plan carefully how to pass on their money. Who’s planning how to pass on their stories? The stories that used to come up at Sunday dinner now need a reason - and a way - to be told.
