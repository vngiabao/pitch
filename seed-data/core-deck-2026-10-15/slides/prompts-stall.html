~0:25 · Habit research found a median of 66 days of daily repetition to form a habit. Weekly prompts allow only about ten attempts in that time, and many only move forward the first few before abandoning as life gets busy. And as the book is completed, the purpose is also gone. This goes against the philosophy of the valuable everyday. Source: Lally et al. 2010, European Journal of Social Psychology.
