Modeled, not observed. First-year contribution per account, before acquisition and fixed costs: $77 at 25% of minutes used, $61 at 50%, $28 at 100% on our component voice stack. On a managed-agent stack it’s $53, $13 and a loss of $69 - so full use on a managed stack doesn’t work at today’s prices. At 50% use, out of $141: $8.79 payment fees, $32.74 voice, $14.40 hosting, $18 support, $6 archive reserve, about $37 acquisition plus trial, leaving $24.12 before fixed costs. At full use that turns to about minus $8.61. Assumptions: 80% Individual / 20% Family plan mix; 2026 voice pricing.
