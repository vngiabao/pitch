Guided calls already exist, and we respect that. Storyworth advertises Story Calls with follow-ups and Family Calls that keep two voices. Remento lets collaborators add questions, photos and their own stories. Storii schedules up to three calls a week. Spomen does follow-ups and multiple perspectives. So phone calls and family contributions are becoming category features. Where we win is the family workflow: scheduled calls that come to the storyteller, every relative’s version kept side by side and credited, and what relatives add shaping the next call - so the story keeps going after the first year instead of ending with the book. That last piece is our core design and what we’re measuring now, not a proven edge yet. “Not stated” means the company’s public pages don’t say either way.
