~0:50 · Meet A Story, an AI biographer that calls you and your loved ones and tells your stories in your own voice. Here’s how it works. It calls at the time you picked, so it’s easy to come back. It listens and adapts to the actual conversation. There’s no fixed script. After each call, it saves the original voice and transcript, so the family can revisit them. Then it recalls. The interviewer remembers past conversations and picks up unfinished stories. Each memory shapes the next call. That’s how a life gets kept while it’s still being lived, one call at a time, as a habit. And any branch of the story can be printed for birthdays, anniversaries or family gatherings. Calls, the archive, photo uploads and billing are live today.
