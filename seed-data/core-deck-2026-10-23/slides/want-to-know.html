~0:30 · Imagine your future kids only know you as their parents. They don’t know how ambitious or adventurous you were in your twenties. Those stories don’t come out on request, and they’re lost if no one tells them. For adult children, this isn't a niche problem. 79% want to know more about their parents’ lives before parenthood. Only 37% know their mother’s life before she became a parent. Most of her story before you is a part no one asked about. And 74% regret not learning more about relatives who have died. By then, it’s too late to ask.
