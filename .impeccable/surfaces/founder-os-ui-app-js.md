# Founder OS: Bookbound redesign (October 14, 2026)

Mode: Operate. Whole-app replacement of the visual world (Today, Funding, Applications, Pitch Studio, Materials, Company facts, Decisions, run and detail views). Product truth, routes, records, approvals and the individual-confirmation boundary are unchanged.

Audience and job: Bao, A Story co-founder, opening pitch.gbaonvo.com each morning after the 9 AM report (laptop and phone). Job: see which money is actually on the table, what closes first, what he already applied to, and what needs his decision; then write or rehearse the pitch.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A founder's working book, not a SaaS dashboard. Today is a contents page of money: what pays cash, what closes first, what is already submitted. It refuses the category default of equal white cards, a teal accent and score badges.

OWN-WORLD: The owner's gbaonvo identity ("The Turn"): Michigan navy bookcloth rail (#00274c) with maize active marks, warm paper ground (#f5f1e8) and soft reading surfaces, brass hairlines and brass-text numerals, ink #0d2238. Mona Sans for interface and data (tabular numerals), Brygada 1918 roman for page titles, program names and the wordmark. Navy pill actions with a circular arrow capsule. Hairline rules, no card shadows.

STORY: The visitor sees cash first, ordered by deadline, with credits as a separate cost-cutting list and a tracker of what was applied, rejected or pending; uncertainty (unknown amount, unverified eligibility) stays in words. They open a program, decide, or jump to the pitch.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy rail left (The Turn mark, A Story / Founder OS wordmark, grouped navigation, Run research). Main: a dated page title, a one-line state sentence, then the dominant "Money on the table" contents list: program in Brygada, dotted leader, amount and days-left right aligned, a deadline hairline sized to days remaining; closing-soon rows marked in oxblood words. Right column: Waiting on you (fact proposals), Applied & tracked (faded rows), the Oct 14 pitch link.

FORM: Bookbound, candidate 5 of 7 (assigned), tuned to the owner's personal palette at the owner's request. Raises: dense tabular figures (from ikeda-datamatics); the one saturated colour only on pressable things and cash (from warm-consumer-app); one dominant area per screen (from zoo-gardens-map); finished programs print faded (from character-goods-catalog); a fixed-scale stage strip found, preparing, applied, decided (from botanical-folio); deadline hairline length equals days left (from labanotation). Signature move: the money table of contents with dot leaders. Motion: 160 to 220 ms ease-out on state, press scale .97, none on keyboard actions. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
