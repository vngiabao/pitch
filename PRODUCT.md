# A Story Founder OS

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Existing Python standard-library domain service and SQLite company store. The local command center is served by that same service; its data and actions use the existing records. This is an extension of the existing Founder OS implementation.

## Users
The A Story founder reviewing opportunities, deciding where to spend time, preparing applications and maintaining exact historical answers and outcomes.

## Product Purpose
Find funding and pitch opportunities worth A Story's time, explain fit and remaining work, and preserve trustworthy application history. Routine research runs automatically through a connected local research runner.

## Operating Context
Original Pitch Competition materials remain in their Windows folder. Canonical company claims, approved answers, submissions, decision receipts, sources and jobs are kept in the existing Founder OS store. The founder uses a command center, opportunity pipeline and decision queue.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Twelve discovery families, including four daily free-credit families, official verification, stable program/cycle identity, weighted scores, unknown eligibility, material readiness, founder effort estimates, deadline intelligence, run reports and retained versions. One owner-confirmed canonical profile contains 15 current company attributes; five migrated essay proposals remain pending. Application Pilot prepares supported free applications using current approved facts. It must email an exact filled preview and obtain the owner's individual confirmation before each submission. Changed fields require another preview. Payments, legal acceptance, confidential program-site uploads and automatic fact promotion are excluded. Connected Gmail now sends owner reports and previews through a scheduled chat at 9 AM Eastern. Real daily-report and question/answer TXT supplement receipts are retained. Owner-only TXT attachments show real known questions, locally filled values, blanks and labeled historical essay drafts. The Materials view searches documents and preserves original downloads/provenance. Local scheduled mail needs the PC awake and Codex running; research/preparation tasks can work with the app closed.

## Brand Commitments
A Story Founder OS; clear founder-oriented language. Extend the original Pitch OS's restrained teal, light surfaces, readable sans typography and familiar sidebar navigation. Preserve original projects and paths.

Owner-selected opportunity email preference: "Black and white - formal and minimal." Opportunity briefs use a monochrome editorial hierarchy, visible practical benefits/deadlines, and separate agent work from owner action. This email-only preference is documented in `.impeccable/email/DESIGN.md`; the Founder OS application retains its existing teal design system.

## Evidence on Hand
The full migration manifest, an expanded library of 1,551 pitch material references (192 with searchable text), 202 immutable registered source versions including owner confirmation, original and revised essays, old competition requirements and deck notes. Historical analyst rejection interpretations are not official program outcomes. Retained research supplies source-linked program observations; screenshots of credit programs are discovery leads rather than proof of current offers.

## Product Principles
Recommendations lead. Uncertainty stays visible. Founder time matters. Current facts govern reuse. Every import and decision remains traceable.

## Pitch-writing preferences
Read `PITCH_WRITING.md` before drafting or adapting pitches and application essays. The owner's October 9 feedback in **NVIDIA Inception Industry Selection** prioritizes distinctive recurring behavior, compounding family context, attributed collaborative perspectives, minimal storyteller effort and original voice. Answer the specific question directly; vendor build questions may start with the needed capabilities instead of a formal company introduction. Use ordinary founder language, one connected paragraph for short answers and normal hyphens. Founder backstories are optional. These are writing preferences and positioning priorities, not approval of new company facts or deployed capabilities.

## Open Decisions
No production hosted deployment or API vendor is selected. This build uses local records and existing Codex research access. Fully offline/powered-off operation requires a later always-on host.
